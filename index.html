Method, data sources & caveats

Data (all open, OGL-licensed): ONS Census 2021 (population TS001, households TS041 per Output Area); ONS Output Area population-weighted centroids & lookups (Open Geography Portal); ONS UK small area GVA estimates 1998–2023 (LSOA, current prices); MHCLG English Indices of Deprivation 2019; Welsh Government WIMD 2019; ONS UK Business Counts via Nomis (local units per MSOA).

How the circle is measured: each Output Area (~125 households) is approximated as a disc around its population-weighted centroid, sized from the distance to its neighbours. The share of that disc inside your coverage circle weights how much of the OA's population, premises, businesses and GVA count as covered. LSOA GVA is allocated to OAs by population and business weight, and business counts are allocated from MSOA by household share — so treat block-level figures as estimates, not facts.

Value model: annual value = (uplift% × covered GVA) + (consumer value × covered residents). The 0.7% default uplift reflects the broad literature on mobile coverage/productivity effects; the honest range is wide (≈0.3–1.5%), hence the slider. 10-year value is discounted at the HM Treasury Green Book rate (3.5%). Assumes genuinely no usable coverage today — where coverage exists, these are upper bounds.

Not included (no free open source exists): real footfall and card-spend data are commercial; business counts and workday GVA are used as proxies.
